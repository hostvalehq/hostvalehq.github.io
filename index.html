Questions hosts ask

Do I need a laptop?

No. Hostvale is made for your phone. Open it in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen like an app.

Does it work with Airbnb and Booking.com?

Yes. Record bookings from any channel and see which one makes you the most money. Automatic Airbnb calendar sync is coming next.

Is my data private?

Each host can only ever see their own bookings and guests. This is enforced by the database itself.

Do guests need to download anything?

No. They open the check-in or booking link you send on WhatsApp.

What if I only have one apartment?

The Starter plan is free for one apartment, with the calendar, payments, caution fees and WhatsApp messages included.
