Questions hosts ask

Do I need a laptop?

No. Hostvale is made for your phone. Open hostvalehq.github.io in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and add it to your home screen. It then opens like any other app.

Does it work with Airbnb and Booking.com?

Yes. Paste your Airbnb or Booking.com calendar link and Hostvale blocks those dates automatically. You also get a Hostvale calendar link to paste back into Airbnb, so their calendar shows your direct bookings too.

How do guests pay me?

Directly to your bank account, the way they already do. Hostvale never holds your money. Your bank details go into the confirmation message, and you record each payment as it arrives.

Do guests need to download anything?

No. Guests open the booking, check-in or review link you send on WhatsApp, in any browser.

Is my data private?

Each host can only ever see their own bookings, guests and money. This is enforced by the database itself, not just the app screens.
