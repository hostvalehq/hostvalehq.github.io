Questions

Good to know

Do I need a laptop?

No. Hostvale is built for your phone first. Open it in your browser and add it to your home screen like an app.

Does it work with Airbnb and Booking.com?

Yes. Record bookings from any channel and Hostvale shows which channel makes you the most money. Automatic calendar sync with Airbnb is coming next.

Is my data safe?

Each host's data is locked to their own account at database level. Nobody else, not even other hosts, can see your bookings or guests.

Do my guests need to download anything?

No. Guests just open the check-in or booking link you send them on WhatsApp.

What if I only have one apartment?

Starter is free forever for one apartment, with the calendar, payments, caution fees and WhatsApp messages included.
